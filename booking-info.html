Pricing & booking questions

How much does an outdoor family session cost?

The 30-minute session is $395 with 15 edited photographs. The 60-minute session is $545 with 25 edited photographs. Each takes place at one outdoor location. Locations outside Chicago’s North Side may involve a travel fee.

How do I book?

Send an inquiry with your preferred session, general location and possible dates. We’ll discuss availability and session details before confirming a booking. An inquiry does not reserve a time.

How will I receive my photographs?

Your selected, edited photographs are delivered as high-resolution digital files by internet download, ready to print and share.

When will my photographs be ready?

Your edited photographs will be ready within 21 calendar days of your session. If you have an earlier deadline, such as a gift or holiday card, please mention it before booking so we can discuss whether it’s possible.

What happens if the weather changes?

Every session is outdoors. If the weather prevents a safe, practical shoot, we’ll arrange a new date with no additional session fee.

Do you offer studio sessions?

No. My family and lifestyle photography sessions take place outdoors.
